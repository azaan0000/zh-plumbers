F-7 plumbing notes — what we actually find

F-7 is one of Islamabad's oldest planned sectors, so the housing stock is split. The big houses off the main roads often still run on the original galvanised piping, and there a leak usually starts at a joint or a corroded elbow rather than in the middle of a straight run. Flats and smaller units around F-7 Markaz are the opposite: newer fittings, but shared walls and shared shafts, so a leak from an upper floor shows up on your ceiling first.

Two things decide the bill here. First, whether the line is original or already partly replaced, because that tells us if we are patching or renewing a length. Second, whether the tank and pump are inside the house or on the roof, since most low-pressure complaints in multi-storey houses trace back to the pump, the float valve or an airlock, not the main line. We confirm all of that before quoting, and the rate is agreed before anything is opened.
