Questions businesses ask

Straight answers before you commit to anything

What is included in the monthly plumbing maintenance plan?▼
A monthly inspection of all bathrooms, kitchens, tanks and pumps; unlimited priority callouts for leaks, blockages and flush failures; geyser and water pump servicing; and one consolidated monthly invoice instead of a separate negotiation on every job.
Which businesses use this service?▼
Guest houses, small hotels, student hostels, offices, co-working spaces, restaurants, gyms, schools, clinics and landlords who rent out flats in Rawalpindi and Islamabad.
How fast do you respond to a maintenance callout?▼
Contract clients get first priority — typically 30 to 40 minutes inside Rawalpindi and Islamabad during the day, and a same-night response for a burst pipe or total water failure.
Do you charge per visit or a fixed monthly fee?▼
A fixed monthly fee based on the number of bathrooms, kitchens, tanks and pumps in the building. The fee is agreed after a free site survey, and the parts used are billed at cost with the receipt attached.
Can you work on a building outside Rawalpindi and Islamabad?▼
Yes, Gujjar Khan, Kahuta, Daultala, Chakwal side and the wider Rawalpindi district are covered, with a slightly longer response window depending on distance.
