Common plumbing jobs in Dubai

  • Hard water scale is the number one callout. Scale builds up inside taps, shower heads and geyser elements, so the flow drops and heating takes longer. A descale and element check usually fixes it without replacing the unit.
  • Villas and older apartment towers in Deira, Bur Dubai and Karama often need rooftop tank cleaning, pump repair and water pressure fixes, because water reaches the building at low pressure and the pump does the rest.
  • Bathroom leaks in buildings older than about fifteen years usually come from a failed flexible connector or the concealed line behind the tiles. A damp patch on the ceiling below is the signal to check before the plaster goes.
