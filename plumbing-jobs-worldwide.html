Frequently asked questions

What is ZH Plumbers Network?

ZH Plumbers Network is a job-sharing network for plumbers. We collect plumbing jobs from clients around the world and post them in our jobs group. The first plumber who accepts a job gets it. The client pays the plumber directly. Our fee is 10%, and only on jobs we bring.

Is joining free?

Yes. Joining is completely free. There is no registration fee, no monthly charge and no interview. You only pay the 10% network fee after you complete a job that we brought you. Work from your own clients costs nothing.

How do I get my first job?

Sign up on the plumber form, then join the jobs group. When a client posts a plumbing job in your area, it appears in the group. Reply first to claim it. The first plumber to accept is assigned the job and receives the client's contact details.

How much does it cost?

The network fee is 10% of the job value, and it applies only to jobs that ZH brought you. Your own clients are always free. Pakistani plumbers pay the fee on SadaPay. Plumbers outside Pakistan pay via Payoneer.

Which areas do you cover?

The network is worldwide. Jobs come from Pakistan, the UAE, Saudi Arabia, the UK, Canada, the USA, Australia and other countries. Job posts always show the area, the service and the timing, so you only respond to work you can do.

Do I need my own tools and helpers?

Yes. You need your own tools and the ability to reach the client's location on time. A helper is optional — many jobs are single-person work such as leaks, taps, commodes, geysers and water motors. You agree the price with the client before starting.
