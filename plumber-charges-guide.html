Plumber Charges: What Affects the Price

Plumber charges in Pakistan depend on the job, not on a fixed list. A dripping tap, a burst concealed pipe and a full bathroom fitting cost very different amounts. We quote on contact after you describe the problem, and the plumber agrees the final price with you before starting any work.

Six things that change the price

1. The type of fault

A visible leak under a sink is quick work. The same leak behind tiled walls or inside a ceiling needs opening, repair and refitting, so it costs more. Concealed work is always the biggest factor.

2. Time and urgency

An emergency at midnight, or work on a Sunday, costs more than the same job on a weekday morning. If you can wait, say so and you will usually get a better rate.

3. Parts and material

A quality brass mixer costs more than a cheap one, and PPRC pipe costs more than PVC. The plumber tells you the options and the difference before buying anything, so nothing is a surprise.

4. Access and floor

A ground floor job is easier than a fourth floor without a lift, or a roof tank that needs carrying. Extra labour and time show up in the quote, and we tell you that upfront.

5. Number of fixtures

One tap or five taps, one bathroom or three: fittings in the same visit share the travel and setup, so the per-unit cost usually drops when the work is grouped.

6. Follow-up and guarantee

A repair that includes a return visit if the same fault comes back costs a little more than a quick patch. Ask about it before the work starts so you know exactly what you are paying for.

How to get a fast, accurate quote

Send three things on WhatsApp and the quote is usually ready in minutes: a photo or short video of the problem, your exact area, and whether it is urgent or can wait. Photos save a full visit just to look at the fault.

Asking before the work starts

Four questions that prevent almost every dispute: is the visit charge separate, are parts included, what is the total if a second fault appears, and is there a return visit if it leaks again. A good plumber answers all four in one message.

Frequently asked questions

Do you publish a fixed price list?▼
No, and any plumber who gives a fixed price for every job is guessing. The fault, the parts, the access and the timing all change the number. You get a real quote on contact and the plumber confirms it on site before starting.
Is the visit charge separate from the repair?▼
Ask when you contact us and we tell you clearly before anyone travels. Many jobs are quoted as one total, including the visit. If a visit charge applies, you will know the amount first, not after the work.
Why is the same repair cheaper at a neighbour's house?▼
Usually access, material or timing. The neighbour may have had a visible leak with a standard part, on a weekday, with three other jobs in the same building. Ask for a breakdown and the difference is almost always explained.
Can I get the price without a visit?▼
Yes for most common jobs. Send a photo or video on WhatsApp with your area and we quote before anyone travels. Only concealed or unknown faults need an on-site look, and we tell you that honestly instead of guessing.
