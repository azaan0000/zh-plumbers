Common plumbing jobs in Toronto

  • Frozen and burst pipes are the winter emergency. Pipes running through exterior walls, garages and unheated crawl spaces freeze first, and the repair is usually a thaw plus a section replacement rather than a whole re-pipe.
  • Homes built before about 1950 in older Toronto neighbourhoods often still have galvanised steel or lead service lines. Brown water in the morning or low pressure at the top floor points to the service line, not the fixtures.
  • Basement flooding is usually a failed sump pump, a missing backflow valve, or a floor drain that cannot handle a heavy storm. A check before the spring rain costs less than a finished basement.
