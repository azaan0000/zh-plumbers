Who we are

ZH Plumbers is a plumbing service run from Gujjar Khan, Punjab, Pakistan. We take on repair and installation work in Rawalpindi, Islamabad and Gujjar Khan: leaks, blockages, geysers, water tanks, pumps, bathroom fittings and concealed piping.

We are not an office full of sales people. The work is done by plumbers who have spent years on site in this region, with the tools and spare parts that are actually available here. When a job needs a specialist we bring one in rather than improvise.

How we work

1. Aap batayein

You tell us the area and the problem on WhatsApp or on our find a plumber form. Photos help and cost nothing.

2. Hum rate batayein

For small jobs we quote on the message. For anything bigger we inspect first, then give the final rate before the work starts. No hidden charges after the job.

3. Kaam, phir payment

The plumber arrives, does the job and shows you it works before payment. If something is wrong we come back and fix it.

What we actually do

  • Emergency leak repair and hidden leak detection
  • Drain, kitchen, bathroom and main sewerage line cleaning
  • Geyser repair, replacement and installation (gas and electric)
  • Water tank cleaning, fitting, and pump or motor installation and repair
  • Bathroom renovation plumbing, concealed PPRC piping, hot and cold lines
  • Commode, flush tank, mixer, shower and basin fitting

The plumber network

Since 2026 we also run a small plumber network. Plumbers in Rawalpindi and Islamabad register with us for free, and when a client job comes in we send it to the plumber who can reach it first. The client pays the plumber directly at the normal market rate.

The network charge is 10 percent and it is paid by the plumber, only on jobs that ZH brought in. Nothing is added to your bill for it, and a plumber's own clients never pay us anything.

Where we are honest about limits

We serve Rawalpindi, Islamabad and Gujjar Khan. We do not currently have teams in other cities, so we do not advertise them. If you are outside our area, message us anyway and we will say plainly whether we can help.
