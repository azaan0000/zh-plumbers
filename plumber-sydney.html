Common plumbing jobs in Sydney

  • Hidden leaks are the expensive ones. A silent leak under a slab or in a garden line can add a large amount to a water bill before anything looks wet, so a meter test is the first step: read it, wait two hours with nothing running, read it again.
  • Older suburbs still have copper lines that pinhole with age, and the repair is a cut-and-solder section rather than a whole re-pipe. A damp patch at the base of a wall is the usual first sign.
  • Blocked stormwater drains cause the flooding everyone complains about, and the cause is usually leaves and roots at the boundary trap rather than the street main. Clearing that point is quick if it is found early.

The network lists real plumbing businesses across Sydney and Greater Sydney, and GPS search on Find Plumbers Near Me shows the nearest. Yes, plumbing work in New South Wales requires a licensed plumber, so ask for the licence number and insurance before work starts.

What is the quickest way to check for a leak?

Turn every tap off, read the water meter, wait two hours, then read it again. If the dial moved, there is a leak. That test costs nothing and tells a plumber whether to look inside the house or at the line outside.
