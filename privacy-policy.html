Privacy Policy

Effective 10 November 2026. Applies to this website and to enquiries sent to ZH Plumbers on WhatsApp, phone or email.

What we collect

  • When you request a plumber: your name, phone number, area, the service you need and any details or photos you send.
  • When you register as a plumber: your name, phone number, city, areas you cover, services, experience and any notes you add.
  • Basic technical data: our hosting provider records standard request logs. Language choice is stored in your own browser only, not on our server.

Why we use it

  • To find the right plumber for your area and send the job to them.
  • To quote a rate, confirm timing and follow up on a job.
  • To keep a record of which plumber took which job, which is what the network fee is calculated from.

Who sees it

Your phone number and job details are shared with the plumber assigned to your job, and with nobody else. We do not sell data, we do not run advertising networks on this site, and we do not add you to any marketing list. Network plumbers' details are visible only to us, not to other plumbers or the public.

How long we keep it

Lead and job records are kept for up to 12 months so a guarantee claim or a fee dispute can still be checked. After that they are deleted. You can ask us to delete your record earlier.

Your choices

You can ask what we hold about you, ask for a correction, or ask us to delete it by emailing azaanbk29@gmail.com or messaging 0370-5840165 on WhatsApp. We will action deletion requests unless we are required to keep a record for an open dispute.

Third-party services we use

  • WhatsApp (Meta) when you message us, under their own privacy terms.
  • Google Search Console and Google Business Profile, to see how people find the business.
  • GitHub Pages, which hosts this website and keeps standard server logs.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above changes with it. Questions about privacy can go to the same email address.
