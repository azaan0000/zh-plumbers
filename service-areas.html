Where we work

Rawalpindi, Islamabad and Gujjar Khan. Below is every area we cover, the issues that come up most in each one, and what our plumbers carry for that kind of housing.

Rawalpindi

Satellite Town

Older houses with long runs of original piping, so slow leaks and joint failures are the usual jobs here, along with blocked kitchen lines in shared kitchens.

Saddar

Commercial buildings and shops. The pattern here is overflow tanks, washroom lines in busy buildings, and drain work that has to happen outside business hours.

Chaklala Scheme 3

Close to the airport, mostly family homes. Water pressure complaints and geyser replacement in winter are the two most frequent calls.

Westridge

Cantonment-side housing with older sewerage connections. Main line blockages and boundary-wall pipe damage are common.

Adiala Road

Mixed housing and small commercial units. Long supply runs mean pump and motor problems show up more often than inside city.

Not in your city?

We do not advertise cities we cannot reach. We have no team in Lahore, Karachi, Dubai or anywhere outside Rawalpindi and Islamabad, so those pages are gone from this site. If you are outside our area, message us and we will tell you honestly whether we can help or whether you should call someone local.

Plumbers outside Rawalpindi and Islamabad are still welcome to register on the network. As the client base grows city by city, registered plumbers there get the work first.
