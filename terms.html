Terms of Service

Effective 10 November 2026. Written in plain English on purpose.

1. What we do

ZH Plumbers provides plumbing repair and installation in Rawalpindi, Islamabad and Gujjar Khan, and runs a network that connects clients with plumbers in those areas. These terms cover both.

2. Quotes and rates

  • A rate given on WhatsApp or phone is an estimate until the job is inspected, unless we wrote it as a fixed rate.
  • The final rate is confirmed before work starts. If the scope changes mid-job, work pauses until you agree the new rate.
  • Parts and fixtures are priced separately and shown to you before purchase.
  • Rates can change if access is blocked, or if the fault turns out to be in a line we were not told about.

3. Payment

You pay the plumber directly, at the end of the job, after you have seen the work. We do not take card payments on this site and we do not add any booking fee to your bill.

4. The network fee (plumbers only)

  • The network charge is 10 percent of the job value.
  • It applies only to jobs that ZH brought to you. Your own clients are yours and cost you nothing.
  • It is paid by the plumber, not by the client. Nothing is added to the client's bill.
  • It becomes due after the job is completed and paid. Payment is by SadaPay to the account number shared at registration.
  • Registration is free. There is no monthly fee and no charge for being listed.

5. Workmanship follow-up

If the same fault returns within 7 days of a repair, tell us and we come back to check it at no labour charge. Parts carry whatever warranty their manufacturer gives. Work on materials you supplied yourself, or damage caused by someone else afterwards, is not covered. Existing pages on this site describe this as a guarantee; this clause is what that means in practice.

6. Cancellations

Cancel any time before the plumber reaches you and there is nothing to pay. If the plumber has already bought parts specifically for your job, those parts are charged at cost.

7. What we are not responsible for

  • Damage that was already there before we arrived.
  • Failures caused by water supply, gas supply or building faults outside the plumbing we worked on.
  • Work done by another plumber after us.

8. Your responsibilities

Give safe access to the area, tell us about hidden pipes or wiring you know of, and confirm you are authorised to order work on the property.

9. Complaints

Message 0370-5840165 or email azaanbk29@gmail.com with the date and the area. We answer complaints within 24 hours. These terms are governed by the laws of Pakistan.
